Tech inside

ten parts.
one quiet system.

Nothing flashy on the spec sheet, every part here earns its place in a sole thin enough to still feel like a shoe.

ESP32-C3 SUPERMINI

The shoe's brain. Runs step logic and talks to the app over WiFi.

MPU-6050

Accelerometer and gyroscope. Detects lift, swing, and ground contact.

DRV2605L

Haptic driver. Times the vibration pattern precisely.

D34 VIBRATION MOTOR

Delivers the massage the moment you hit your goal.

TPS63020 BUCK-BOOST

Keeps power stable even as the battery runs down.

2N2222 TRANSISTOR

Switches motor power on and off on command.

1KΩ RESISTOR

Stabilizes current so the motor runs smoothly.

2.2ΜF CAPACITOR

Smooths power delivery to keep signals clean.

3.7V 1200MAH LIPO

Powers a full day of steps on a single charge.

WEMOS CH340G USB

Handles charging and lets the team reprogram the shoe.
